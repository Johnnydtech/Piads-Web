## What "free" usually costs

The recurring pattern with free setups isn't the software — it's these:

- **Your time.** Imaging Pi SD cards, debugging a TV browser that sleeps, walking USB sticks around: hours every month, forever.
- **The missing remote update.** The entire point of digital signage is changing the screen from your phone in seconds. USB-stick and photo-app setups give that up — you've built a static poster with extra steps.
- **No scheduling.** Breakfast → lunch → happy hour switching is where signage earns its keep. Free setups rarely have it.
- **No health monitoring.** A screen that quietly died on Saturday costs more than a subscription.
