Every venue has announcements — new hours, this week's events, a staff shout-out, "card machine is down." The wall of taped paper works until it doesn't get read. Here's how to put announcements on a TV instead, from the duct-tape methods to the one that runs itself.

## Three ways, honestly compared

### 1. USB stick + the TV's photo app (the duct-tape way)

Export announcement images, load them on a USB stick, use the TV's built-in slideshow. **Works** for a static set of slides. **Fails** the moment anything changes — every update means re-exporting, finding the stick, and walking to the TV. Most photo apps also sleep, show filenames, or reset order after power cuts.

### 2. Casting / HDMI from a laptop

Screen-mirror from a phone or park a laptop on HDMI. Fine for a one-off event. As a daily system it means a device held hostage by the TV, and the display dies when that device sleeps, updates, or leaves the building.

### 3. Signage software (the runs-itself way)

A small player (a [Fire TV Stick](/blog/fire-tv-stick-digital-signage-setup-guide) — or even [just a browser](/blog/browser-based-digital-signage)) plays a playlist you control from a web dashboard:

- Post an announcement **from your phone**; it's on screen in seconds
- **Schedule** it — the event promo runs this week and removes itself
- Mix announcements with your menu, promos, and photos in one rotation
- The screen recovers from power cuts by itself and shows you it's online

## Making announcements people actually read

- **One message per slide.** Three announcements = three slides, 8–12 seconds each — not one crowded poster.
- **Big text.** Readable from across the room means headline sizes that look absurd in the editor. They're right.
- **Give slides an expiry.** Schedule the end date when you post — nothing rots trust in a board like April's event in June.
- **Keep a rhythm.** Announcements between menu/promo slides get seen more than an announcements-only loop people learn to ignore.
- **Templates:** a Canva template per announcement type (event / hours / shout-out) makes each new one a 2-minute job. ([Canva → TV guide](/blog/canva-digital-signage-display-on-tv).)
