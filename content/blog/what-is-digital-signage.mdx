Digital signage is a TV (or any screen) showing content **you** control — your menu, your specials, your announcements — instead of cable TV or a blank wall. That's the whole idea. This guide explains the four pieces, what they cost, and how modern venues set it up in an afternoon.

## The four pieces

Every digital signage setup, from a single cafe TV to an airport, is the same four parts:

1. **The screen** — usually a normal TV. Commercial displays exist for 24/7 outdoor duty, but for most venues the TV you own is exactly right.
2. **The player** — the small computer that puts content on the screen: a Fire TV Stick, an Android device, a Raspberry Pi, or just a web browser. This is where old-school vendors sell you $300 boxes; you almost never need one.
3. **The software** — the dashboard where you upload content, build playlists, and schedule what plays when. This is what you're actually choosing when you "pick digital signage."
4. **The content** — menu images, promo graphics, videos. Most venues make theirs in Canva in minutes.

## What venues actually use it for

- **Menu boards** that switch from breakfast to lunch automatically ([restaurants](/digital-signage-for/restaurants), [cafes](/digital-signage-for/cafes-coffee-shops))
- **Promos and specials** without printing anything
- **Schedules** — class timetables at [gyms](/digital-signage-for/gyms-fitness-studios), service menus at [salons](/digital-signage-for/salons-barbershops)
- **Announcements** — events, hours, new arrivals, staff shout-outs
- **Waiting-room content** at [medical and dental offices](/digital-signage-for/medical-dental-offices)
- And on PiAds specifically: **earning** — renting spare screen time to local advertisers

## FAQ

**Do I need a "smart" TV?**
No — any TV with HDMI. The player (stick or browser device) provides the smarts.

**Do I need design skills?**
No. Canva templates plus your photos cover 95% of venue content. See [how to display a Canva design on your TV](/blog/canva-digital-signage-display-on-tv).

**What happens when the internet goes down?**
Good platforms cache content on the player and keep playing offline, syncing again when the connection returns. PiAds does.

**What's the difference between digital signage and just casting?**
Control and reliability. Casting mirrors a device ad-hoc; signage runs scheduled playlists unattended, all day, recovering on its own — no phone needed, nothing to babysit.
