The Fire TV Stick is quietly the best digital signage player for small venues — and there's a good chance one is already plugged into your TV. This guide covers exactly which sticks work, the 10-minute setup, what it costs, and the mistakes to avoid.

Some signage vendors steer you away from Fire TV Sticks toward their own hardware. Our take: for a cafe, gym, salon, or shop running menus and promos 12 hours a day, a modern stick is more than enough — and the economics are unbeatable when the hardware is already behind your TV.

## Which Fire TV devices work

PiAds runs natively on the **entire Fire TV family**:

- **Fire TV Stick 4K / 4K Max** — the sweet spot: smooth video, Wi-Fi 6, ~$45–60
- **Fire TV Stick HD / Lite** — fine for image-based menus and promos, ~$30
- **Fire TV Cube** — overkill but excellent, wired ethernet option
- **Fire TV televisions** — no stick needed at all
- **New Vega OS devices** — Amazon's next-generation Fire TV platform; PiAds ships a native Vega app, so new-generation sticks work out of the box

Rule of thumb: video-heavy content → get the 4K. Static menus and images → any stick you own is fine.

## The 10-minute setup

1. **Plug the stick into the TV** and connect it to your Wi-Fi (a normal Fire TV first-run flow).
2. **Install the PiAds app** — search "PiAds" in the Amazon Appstore on the device.
3. **Open the app.** A 6-character pairing code appears on the TV.
4. **Pair it** — in [app.piads.co](https://app.piads.co), go to Screens → Connect screen, enter the code, name the screen ("Front counter").
5. **Add content** — the guided setup builds your first playlist and schedule; from then on, everything you publish appears on the TV in seconds.

No hardware to image, no cables beyond the TV's, no ladder if the stick's already mounted.

## Settings worth changing for signage duty

- **Disable the screensaver** (Settings → Display & Sounds) so your content never gets replaced by Amazon's photo slideshow.
- **Turn off auto sleep** where offered, or set the TV input to stay awake — signage should survive a quiet afternoon.
- **Use 5GHz Wi-Fi** if your router offers it; menus update faster and video streams cleaner.
- **Plug into mains power**, not the TV's USB port — TV USB sometimes can't power a 4K stick reliably.

## Common questions

**Does the content keep playing if the internet drops?**
Yes — the PiAds player caches content and keeps playing, then re-syncs when the connection returns.

**Can I still use the TV normally?**
Yes. PiAds is just an app — exit it and the Fire TV works as usual. Most venues leave it running during opening hours.

**Fire TV Stick vs Raspberry Pi?**
Pi is great for 24/7 dual-screen setups if you enjoy the tinkering; the stick wins on price, availability, and zero-maintenance. Full comparison: [Raspberry Pi vs Fire TV vs Android](/blog/raspberry-pi-vs-fire-tv-vs-android-digital-signage).
