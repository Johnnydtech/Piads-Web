# AppSumo Listing Draft — PiAds

## Tagline
Turn the TVs you already own into digital signage — and into local ad revenue.

## One-liner
PiAds is a dead-simple digital signage CMS: pair any TV in under a minute, upload media, build playlists, schedule by daypart — and where the PiAds ad marketplace is live, keep 70% when local businesses advertise on your screens.

## Description
Every gym, café, salon, and coworking space has a TV on the wall doing nothing. PiAds puts it to work in three steps:

1. **Pair any screen in under a minute.** A pairing code connects the free web player, a Fire TV stick, Android TV, or a Raspberry Pi. No proprietary hardware.
2. **Show your own content.** Drag-and-drop images and video into one media library, stack them into playlists (mix in YouTube, websites, and social embeds), and schedule different playlists for breakfast, lunch, and evening on a visual weekly grid.
3. **Earn when locals advertise.** Where the PiAds marketplace is live, open the ad slots you choose to nearby businesses. You approve every ad, and you keep 70% of every booking.

Unlike enterprise signage platforms, there are no tiers to decode and no per-feature upsells — the whole CMS is included.

## FAQ seeds
- **Do I need special hardware?** No — any TV plus a browser, Fire TV stick, Android TV, or Raspberry Pi.
- **Is the ad revenue guaranteed?** No. The 70% share applies where advertiser demand exists in your region (rolling out market by market, starting in Arlington, VA). The signage CMS works everywhere, worldwide, today.
- **What counts as a screen?** One paired player device.
- **What's excluded?** The advertiser side (buying ads) is a separate, always-recurring product.
