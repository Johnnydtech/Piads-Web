# Review Request Email

**Trigger:** 7 days after a venue's first screen comes online (screen status = online, account age ≥ 7 days). Send once. Skip if an open support ticket exists.

**From:** PiAds <support@piads.co>
**Subject:** Quick favor? (30 seconds, means a lot)

---

Hi {{first_name}},

Your screen at {{venue_name}} has been live for a week now — hope it's already earning its spot on the wall.

I have a small ask: would you leave PiAds a short review? Honest words from real venue owners are how other local businesses find us, and it genuinely takes about 30 seconds.

**[Leave a review on Trustpilot]({{trustpilot_url}})**

If anything's *not* working the way you expected, reply to this email instead — I read every reply and I'd rather fix it than have you write around it.

Thanks for running your screens with us,
The PiAds team

---

**Variants:**
- LTD buyers: same email, day 14, subject "How's the lifetime deal treating you?" — AppSumo requires reviews on their marketplace, so link the AppSumo product page instead of Trustpilot for those customers.
- G2/Capterra: once Trustpilot has 10+ reviews, alternate the destination link to build presence on a second platform.
